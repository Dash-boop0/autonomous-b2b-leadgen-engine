# Competitor notes (SYNTHETIC; fictional vendors invented for this demo)

## Valemont Analytics (fictional)
Enterprise-focused planning suite; long implementations; pricing aimed at 1000+ staff companies.
Gap: mid-market buyers without a data team are under-served.

## ForecastFox (fictional)
Self-serve forecasting app for e-commerce merchants; strong Shopify integration; little support for multi-depot
wholesale or logistics capacity planning.
Gap: no warehouse/labour capacity view for logistics operators.

## Spreadsheet status quo
Most target accounts plan in spreadsheets maintained by one analyst.
Gap: key-person risk and no audit trail of forecast changes.
