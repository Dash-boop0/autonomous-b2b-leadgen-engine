# Market notes (SYNTHETIC, written for this demo; not real market research)

## Logistics
Mid-market third-party logistics firms in GB, IE and NL report volatile volumes and difficulty planning labour and
warehouse space. Several are opening new distribution centres. Forecasting is usually spreadsheet-based.

## Wholesale Distribution
Wholesale distributors with 50-1000 staff carry thousands of SKUs; stockouts and excess stock both hurt margin.
ERP migrations are a common trigger to revisit planning tools.

## Manufacturing
Discrete manufacturers in the 50-1000 staff range plan production from customer forecasts that are often late.
Demand-sensing tools are evaluated alongside ERP upgrades.

## Retail
Large retailers usually have in-house forecasting teams and long procurement cycles; mid-market fit is weaker.

## Food & Beverage
Perishable inventory makes forecast accuracy valuable, but many buyers are already served by specialist vendors.
