# Northwind Analytics (fictional) - approved customer-facing statements v1.0 (SYNTHETIC)

## product-overview
Northwind Forecast produces weekly SKU-level demand forecasts from historical order data and shows the expected
stock position for each depot.

## integrations
Northwind Forecast imports data from CSV extracts and from scheduled exports of common ERP systems; no direct ERP
write-back is offered.

## data-security
Customer data is processed in an EU-hosted environment and access is restricted by role; a security overview
document is available on request.

## onboarding
A typical pilot uses 12 months of order history and runs for one product category first.
